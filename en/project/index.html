In ProgressInteractive Viewer

4D Rubik’s Cube Viewer

I am developing a viewer for exploring and manipulating a 4D Rubik’s Cube. The project investigates how an invisible four-dimensional state can first be represented and controlled in three-dimensional space. As a foundation, I am also prototyping ways to communicate 3D structure and viewpoint on a 2D screen.
