Research (JA) About Me

Research Overview

My current research examines how shifts in body ownership induced by the Rubber Hand Illusion affect movements guided by perceived body position.

I also study perception and cognition through phenomena such as the Phantom Touch Illusion and body ownership in VR, including how such shifts influence human behavior and movement.
